# Copenhagen Alliance standard versifications

`eng.json`, `rsc.json` and `org.json` are unmodified copies of the standard versification
mappings published by the Copenhagen Alliance:

https://github.com/Copenhagen-Alliance/versification-specification/tree/5f3f82f3dc3cfd25fffc6ff04f3630763972258c/versification-mappings/standard-mappings

They are used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
(CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Source import uses them to recognise which versification a source's text follows, and to
fill gaps inside a chapter where the text leaves out a verse.
